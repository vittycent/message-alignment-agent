# Prepare your inputs

Do this before session 1, in an hour or two over a couple of days. It is mostly collecting things you already have. Each section ends with a **Your turn** box. Please fill those in. They are what makes the agent yours.

## 1. Your company brief (start here)

Create one page, `context/company-brief.md`. Session 0 will help you finish it, but start with a few lines. Use `templates/company-brief.md`.

**Your turn.** Answer in your own words:

- What do you sell, to whom, and what is the main job it does for them?
- Who are your competitors, and how does a rep describe the difference in one sentence?
- What are your three core messages?
- What are your buying stages? (A simple set works: qualification, discovery, evaluation, onboarding, review and renewal.)
- What does "renewal" mean for you? Annual contract? Usage? Who owns it?
- Which words does your team use that need explaining?

## 2. Transcripts

| What | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Volume to start** | **8 to 12 calls** for the first week. A first trial can use as few as 2 to 3 |
| **Volume for a useful report** | **3 or more weeks**, 8 to 12 calls a week. One week shows direction, three weeks shows patterns |
| **Length** | 20 to 40 minutes. Very short calls give little evidence, very long ones need to be split |
| **Mix** | Include the stages that matter most to you. Customer success and renewal calls are the most useful, and usually the least supported |
| **Format** | Text or Markdown, one speaker turn per line, with a timestamp: `[00:12:31] **Name:** what they said` |
| **Metadata** | Date, call type or stage, the names of people on your team, and the company name |
| **Where they live** | Gong, Notion, Drive or your meeting tool. Export them to a folder, or tell Claude Code where to read them |

**Your turn.**
- Which calls will you start with, and why?
- Who on each call is on your team? (Claude needs this to know who counts as "us".)
- Are there calls you would rather not send to a model? Remove them or redact names.

## 3. The enablement assets

| What | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Which** | Everything a rep or CSM can use or send: decks, one pagers, blogs, scripts, templates, playbooks, webinars, positioning docs |
| **Volume to start** | 5 assets is enough for a trial. A full register is usually 15 to 40 asset *versions* |
| **Versions** | Include old versions that are still in circulation. They matter |
| **Format** | Text or Markdown with numbered sections or slides. Export slides to text, one slide per section |
| **Dates** | When each version was released, and when it was replaced. Approximate is fine |
| **Owner and audience** | Who owns it, who it is for, and at which buying stage |

**Your turn.** Make a list of your assets with these columns:

`name, version, released, replaced (if any), owner, who it is for, buying stage, where it lives`

Then answer: which ones do you **suspect are not used**? Which are **used but should not be**?

## 4. Context files (you probably have most of these)

- Positioning and messaging document
- Ideal customer profile and personas
- Messaging hierarchy, if you have one
- Competitor battlecards
- Customer proof points and quotes
- A glossary of your own terms
- Your org chart for sales and customer success (who is rep, who is CSM)

**Your turn.** Rank them by how much you trust them. A positioning doc written two years ago and never updated is useful too, but tell Claude it is old.

## 5. Access and privacy

- **Where is your data?** Claude Code needs to read the files. Check how you can export from Gong, Notion or Drive.
- **Can your transcripts be sent to Claude?** Check your company's rules on customer data and call recordings. If in doubt, redact customer names first.
- **Keep an `internal/` folder.** Anything the agents should *not* read (answer keys, your private notes) goes in a folder the agents are told never to open. The kickoff file sets this up.
- **Credits.** Know your Claude Code usage limits. A full week of readings can take hundreds of thousands of tokens. Start small.

**Your turn.** Write down three things you would not want any agent to read, and tell Claude Code on day one.

## 6. A quick checklist

- [ ] A one page company brief (even rough)
- [ ] 5 to 40 assets in text form, with a list of versions and dates
- [ ] 2 to 3 calls to start, 8 to 12 for the first week
- [ ] Your positioning document, and any messaging hierarchy
- [ ] A colleague for session 0
- [ ] A Claude Code project folder with this kit in it
- [ ] Your usage limits, and a plan for credits
- [ ] Eight short blocks in your calendar over about two weeks

When you have ticked most of these, give Claude Code [kickoff/START-HERE-for-claude-code.md](../kickoff/START-HERE-for-claude-code.md).
