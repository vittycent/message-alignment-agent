# Report first screen (example from the fictional Rootline data)

This is the first screen of Victor's master report after three weeks of fictional calls. The company, the customers, and every quote are made up.

## First screen

1. **Reduction planning is now why customers leave, and reps are promising what the roadmap says not to promise.** In W34, all three renewal and exit conversations (Nordkost, Bergstrand, Aalborg) had the missing pathway as a reason not to renew: "We left over the pathway" (Aalborg, 00:34:02), "Something I can put in front of the full board in December that answers the question they asked" (Bergstrand, 00:54:38). Across W33 and W34, 8 calls ask for reduction planning. Meanwhile reps in 4 W34 calls (Nordflor, Karnhuset, Bergstrand, Sydost) describe scenario comparison as available, give a quarter ("Q1") or a month, or park the pathway as "phase two", where DECK-4 S21 says in design, give no date and POS-2 limits scenarios to single product what ifs. *High: both readings, both weeks.*
2. **Renewal is managed by hope, and usage goes unseen.** Nobody asked the would you renew question in any of the three W33 and W34 business reviews and renewal calls (Havsfisk, Nordkost, Bergstrand). At Bergstrand the CSM asked what would keep them once, 54 minutes into a 65 minute call. At Aalborg a customer stopped using the product ("four logins in nine months", the fourteen brand managers "never once opened it") and learned the licences were unused only "when I went to cancel". The review template has a usage section and a notice date line; neither was used (Aalborg, Bergstrand). *High for the pattern; four calls, so direction not measurement.*
3. **Customers still buy because their customer asked, and the champion is left to carry it internally.** W33: 7 of 10 calls start from a customer's data request, most with a date. W34: 5 of 11, in 3 of them with the consequence under it ("Then we are the supplier who did not answer", Smakbolaget, 00:17:51). In both weeks champions need to make the case to their MD, board or CFO (6 of 11 calls in W34, both readings in 5) and nothing equips them: "I got the content in a conversation I could not forward to my board." (Aalborg, 00:24:29). *High: both readings, both weeks.*
