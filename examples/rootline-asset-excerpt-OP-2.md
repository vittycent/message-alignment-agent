## 4. Why Rootline

- **Built only for food and beverage.** Most of our footprint is in purchased goods, and Rootline measures them at the ingredient, by origin and farming practice
- **A number we can defend.** Every figure traces to its source, and every line shows whether it is primary data or an average
- **We own the model.** Cheaper than a consultant from year two, and we can correct and rerun it ourselves
- **The next request starts from a base.** Product and corporate footprints come from one model, so a second customer, a bank or an auditor doesn't mean starting again
- **It leads to decisions.** Supplier level numbers show where reduction in our sourcing is possible
